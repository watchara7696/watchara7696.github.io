About Me

Hosting a lecture on special relativity and Lorentz transformation during school open day in Grade 10

My name is Michael. I am a rising senior high school student based in Wuhan, China. Physics is my core academic passion. I have independently studied most foundational university-level physics courses, including classical mechanics, electromagnetism, thermodynamics, optics, analytical mechanics, electrodynamics, and partial quantum mechanics.

I once attended Harvard University’s summer program: Introduction to Digital Fabrication. This course equipped me with practical engineering skills, including Fusion 360 design, 3D printing, circuit layout, wireless electronic control, and motor system programming. Before this experience, my physics learning was limited to theoretical study from textbooks; the program encouraged me to combine physical theories with hands-on prototype building.

I maintain deep concern for societal and public health challenges. Inspired by my father’s conditions of hyperlipidemia, hypertension, gout, as well as my long-term volunteer experience at rehabilitation centers, I started designing practical assistive devices targeting daily rehabilitation needs. I aim to translate conceptual ideas into functional creations that deliver tangible benefits to people.

This website archives my personal projects, combining theoretical physics thinking and digital manufacturing practice. I intend to continue exploring physics and related interdisciplinary engineering research in college and future career.
